# Product

- Product: Personal developer portfolio, designed to evolve into a reusable template.
- Goal: Present professional experience, technical skills, projects and contact links.
- Audience: Recruiters, engineering managers and potential clients.
- Primary language: English for portfolio UI; Portuguese for developer communication unless requested otherwise.
- Priorities: Accurate content, strong visual hierarchy, accessibility, responsiveness and performance.
- Source of truth for professional facts: `.kiro/specs/portfolio-context.md`.
- Never invent employment history, achievements, dates or qualifications.
- Do not copy personal contact details into unrelated files or documentation.
- V1 scope: Functional personal portfolio. No CMS, authentication, i18n or multi-template system.
- Template readiness: Separate content from presentation. Components must not contain hardcoded personal data.
